# Launches

The next rockets to fly, one card each: the rocket on its pad, the mission, a
countdown and its status (GO, TBC, HOLD), and where it flies from.

**Launches you can see come first.** Anything from a site within your distance
(Vandenberg reaches most of California) shows how far away it is and which way
to look. Launches between sunset and full dark get a **twilight** flag: that is
when the exhaust high up is still lit by the Sun against a dark sky, and the
whole of Southern California goes outside to look at the "jellyfish".

**Ten minutes before one of those**, the screen takes over: the count with the
rocket venting on the pad, the last ten seconds, liftoff, and the trail arcing
downrange with the time since launch and where to look.

Launch times from [The Space Devs](https://thespacedevs.com/) Launch Library 2 (free,
no key; checked every 15 minutes, every 6 when one of yours is close).

## Install

Paste this **folder URL** into RackTicker → Plugins → Add a plugin:

```
https://github.com/costamesatechsolutions/rackticker-community-plugins/tree/main/plugins/launches
```

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | home | Where you watch from |
| `count` | `4` | How many launches to show |
| `visible_miles` | `400` | Launch sites this close are yours: shown first, with where to look |
| `alert` | on | Take over the display for a launch you can see |
| `alert_lead_minutes` | `10` | How long before launch the take-over starts |
