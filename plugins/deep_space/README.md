# Deep space

How far away our farthest spacecraft are, rolling like an odometer at the speed
they are really going.

- **Voyager 1**, launched in 1977 and the farthest thing we have made, about
  16 billion miles away and gaining 23 miles every second, with how long its radio
  signal takes to reach us (nearly a day).
- **One light-day**: Voyager 1's countdown to being one light-day from Earth, in
  November 2026, with how close it is now.
- **Voyager 2** and **New Horizons**, past Pluto since 2015.
- **Parker Solar Probe**, the other way: how far it is from the Sun as it loops
  in and out, the fastest object we have built.

Distances from NASA JPL's [Horizons](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/) system,
asked for one spacecraft at a time, each once every six hours. Between those, the
distance is carried forward at the rate Horizons gives, so the odometer is right
to the mile.

## Install

Paste this **folder URL** into RackTicker → Plugins → Add a plugin:

```
https://github.com/costamesatechsolutions/rackticker-community-plugins/tree/main/plugins/deep_space
```
