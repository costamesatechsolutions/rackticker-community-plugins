# Bar trivia

The question board over the bar. A question pages in two lines at a time, the four
answers light up with a countdown bar, then the right one is revealed in green.
Nobody has to play along; it is just something good to look at.

- **Always fresh.** Questions are fetched a few at a time from two free, key-less
  sources and mixed together; a question is not repeated until the pool has been
  through. A built-in set of 35 questions keeps it running with no internet.
- **Set it up** in Settings: categories (sports, film, TV, music, geography, history,
  video games, food & drink, science, ...), difficulty, questions per visit and how
  long the answers stay up. The playlist waits for the question on screen to finish.
- **Legible by design.** Only questions whose four answers fit on one line are shown,
  and questions that depend on a picture or audio are dropped.
