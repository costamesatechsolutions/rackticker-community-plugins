# Casino

Games of chance that play themselves, under the lights. No money, just the show.

- **Plinko**: chips drop from the top and rattle down through seven rows of pegs
  into the prize slots of the TV board ($100, $500, $1,000, $0, $10,000, $0,
  $1,000, $500, $100). Each landing lights its slot, and the winnings add up in the corner.
- **Slots**: three reels spin and stop one after another with a bounce, the pay
  line lights on a win and the credits count up. Two sevens and the third one
  just past the line says SO CLOSE. Three sevens is the jackpot: the bulbs go wild.

## Install

Paste this **folder URL** into RackTicker → Plugins → Add a plugin:

```
https://github.com/costamesatechsolutions/rackticker-community-plugins/tree/main/plugins/casino
```

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mode` | `auto` | `auto` plays Plinko then the slots on each visit; or just `plinko` or `slots` |
