# Sky

Your whole horizon, all 360 degrees, across the panel: north at both ends, south
in the middle (north in the middle south of the equator), the compass along the
bottom.

- **Sky now**: the Sun, the Moon (as bright as it is full), Mercury, Venus, Mars,
  Jupiter and Saturn, and the brightest stars, each at its real bearing and
  height, over a sky coloured by the time of day: blue by day, an orange glow on
  the side the Sun went down, the stars coming out as it gets dark.
- **The next twelve hours in eleven seconds**: watch the Sun set, the planets
  climb and the stars wheel across, with the clock running.
- **What's up**: the Moon and each planet, up now and when it sets, or when it
  rises next.

Worked out on the device, with no internet: planets from JPL's approximate
orbital elements, the Moon from the main terms of its theory, the stars from
their catalogue places. Good to well under a pixel (a pixel is about three degrees).

## Install

Paste this **folder URL** into RackTicker → Plugins → Add a plugin:

```
https://github.com/costamesatechsolutions/rackticker-community-plugins/tree/main/plugins/sky
```

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | home | Where you are looking from; leave it to use the home location from Settings |
